Transfer translator

Learn the meaning first. Then learn the acronym.

These terms are related, but they are not interchangeable. Open only the ones you need.

Transferable vs. articulated

Short version: A course can be transferable without matching a specific university course or major requirement.

Transferable The receiving university may award transfer credit for the course.
Articulated An official agreement explains how a course satisfies a particular course or requirement at another institution.

Do not guess: Check the current agreement in ASSIST when the answer depends on a particular college, university, major, or academic year.

General education vs. major preparation
General education Broad lower-division learning requirements across areas such as communication, humanities, social sciences, and science.
Major preparation Courses that prepare a student specifically for the major they plan to enter.

Finishing general education does not necessarily mean a student has finished major preparation.

AA/AS vs. AA-T/AS-T (ADT)

ADT means Associate Degree for Transfer. California Community Colleges award AA-T and AS-T degrees designed for transfer to the CSU.

The CSU states that students who complete an approved ADT and meet the applicable requirements receive transfer benefits, but the guarantee is not necessarily admission to a particular campus or major.

Read “ADT, Actually” for the plain-language guide · Review the CSU's current Associate Degree for Transfer guidance.

Cal-GETC

Plain English: Cal-GETC is California's current intersegmental lower-division general education pattern for students newly enrolling at a California Community College beginning in fall 2025.

It can satisfy lower-division general education requirements at CSU and most freshman/sophomore-level general education requirements at UC, but it is not the same thing as completing every transfer or major requirement.

Students with earlier enrollment may have different catalog-rights considerations and should verify their pattern with their college.

Read “Cal-GETC, Actually” for the plain-language guide · Review UC's current Cal-GETC guidance.

ASSIST, actually

ASSIST is the official statewide source for California articulation and transferability information.

Use it when you need to know how coursework from a California Community College may satisfy elective, general education, course, or major requirements at participating institutions.

Start with ASSIST, Actually if you want a guided walkthrough before opening the live database.

About the project

Make the invisible system visible.

Transfer, Actually is an independent educational resource designed to make California Community College transfer easier to understand for students and faculty.

It explains concepts, shows relationships, and points people toward authoritative sources. It does not replace academic counseling, articulation review, admissions guidance, or official institutional information.

Design promise

  • Start with the user's question, not institutional vocabulary.
  • Introduce one layer of complexity at a time.
  • Use examples and comparisons to make abstract ideas concrete.
  • Never hide uncertainty when an answer must be verified.
  • Build for keyboard access, reflow, readable contrast, and reduced motion.
