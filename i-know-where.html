A destination-first transfer path

I Know Where I Want to Go. Now What?

A destination gives you something powerful: a place to work backward from. Now find out what that university expects, how your CCC coursework connects, and whether the plan still gives you a realistic backup.

Plan backward, not randomly.

Start at the university and move backward until you reach the classes you should take next.

University + campus ↓ Exact major/program ↓ Admission + major preparation ↓ Articulation + GE ↓ Your next CCC courses

The backward-planning habit

A campus name is the beginning of the plan—not the end of it.

Move through all six checks before deciding that your current course plan “works” for the destination.

1

Program

Name the exact major, concentration, school, or college if relevant.

2

Admission

Check transfer eligibility plus any selective/impacted program standards.

3

Major prep

Find required and recommended lower-division preparation.

4

Articulation

See how courses at your CCC satisfy those requirements.

5

GE + pathway

Fit Cal-GETC and ADT/TSP/TAG or other applicable pathways around the plan.

6

Deadline

Work backward again from the application term and filing dates.

Do not stop at “they accept transfers”

A destination has at least four different questions hiding inside it.

Major

Can I enter the program I actually want?

  • Is the major offered to transfer applicants?
  • Is it selective, capped, screened, or impacted?
  • Are there GPA or course requirements beyond general transfer eligibility?
Admission

What makes me eligible—and competitive?

  • Minimum transfer requirements
  • Campus/program-specific criteria
  • Application term and filing period
Credit

What will my CCC courses actually do there?

  • Transfer credit
  • Major preparation
  • Course-to-course articulation
  • General education
Reality

Could I actually attend if admitted?

  • Cost after aid
  • Commute or housing
  • Work/family constraints
  • Whether a backup destination protects the same major pathway

Where to verify

Your source changes depending on the university.

Do not force every destination through the same tool.

CSU

Use CSU + ASSIST together.

  • Use the CSU Transfer Planner and campus transfer pages for admission planning.
  • Check CSU impaction for the campus and major.
  • Use ASSIST for articulation and major preparation.
  • Check whether ADT or TSP gives you a structured pathway.
UC

Use UC admissions + ASSIST together.

  • Use the campus/UC admission pages for transfer requirements and selection.
  • Use ASSIST for major-preparation articulation.
  • Check TAG if the campus participates and you are eligible.
  • Use UC TAP to organize coursework and TAG planning.
California independent

First see whether the university is in ASSIST.

Participating AICCU institutions have begun publishing agreements in ASSIST. Participation is optional, so the university may or may not appear.

Check participating AICCU institutions.

Out of state / not in ASSIST

Go directly to the receiving university.

ASSIST does not currently publish articulation for out-of-state institutions or private institutions that have not opted into ASSIST. The receiving university has authority over how it evaluates and applies transfer credit.

Right now · September 2026

If you are aiming for fall 2027, some clocks are already running.

This snapshot is deliberately dated. Always use the current official calendar before acting.

UC TAG: September 1–30, 2026

For eligible fall 2027 UC applicants

TAG is separate from the general UC application. A student using TAG still needs to submit the UC application during its filing period.

UC application: November 1–30, 2026

Fall 2027 transfer filing period

The application opened for preparation August 1. The filing period for fall 2027 transfer admission is November 1–30.

CSU application: start now; submit October 1–November 30, 2026

Fall 2027 CSU transfer

Cal State Apply opened early for preparation. CSU says submission begins October 1 and the fall 2027 priority period closes November 30.

CSU TSP: August 1–October 31, 2026

Only for eligible new CCC students

The Transfer Success Pathway has a separate agreement cycle and narrower eligibility than ordinary transfer admission.

Brain-friendly rule: once you pick an intended transfer term, put every major deadline on one page or calendar. Do not rely on remembering that “applications are sometime in fall.”

The part nobody wants to hear

A destination is a goal. It should not be your only plan.

Knowing exactly where you want to go is useful—but selective admissions, impacted majors, cost, geography, and changing circumstances make a one-school strategy fragile.

  • Keep at least one additional destination that works for the same or a closely related major.
  • Compare major preparation early enough that the backup does not require an entirely different set of courses.
  • If your first choice is a highly selective program, ask what happens if you are admitted to the university but not that major.
  • Before application season, compare affordability and travel/housing reality—not just admission requirements.
